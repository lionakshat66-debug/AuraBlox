🚀 START TO END BLOX FRUITS GUIDE

The ultimate progression roadmap from Level 1 to Endgame

🔥 Your Complete Blox Fruits Roadmap

Follow this guide from the beginning of your journey all the way to endgame. Learn what to unlock, what to farm, which upgrades matter and how to build a powerful account.

🌱 Beginner Stage (Level 1-700)

Your goal is reaching the Second Sea as quickly as possible. Do not waste time hunting random NPCs. Always complete quests.

  • Choose Pirate or Marine.
  • Do Starter Island quests.
  • Move to Jungle as soon as possible.
  • Save money for better fruits.
  • Use Light, Ice, Magma or Buddha if possible.
  • Always put most stats into Melee, Defense and Fruit.
  • Avoid spending money on random swords.
  • Unlock Aura (Buso Haki).
  • Unlock Observation Haki.
  • Continue leveling until Level 700.

Best beginner fruit: Buddha.
Best alternatives: Light, Ice, Magma.

🌊 First Sea Island Route

  • Starter Island
  • Jungle
  • Pirate Village
  • Desert
  • Frozen Village
  • Marine Fortress
  • Sky Islands
  • Prison
  • Colosseum
  • Magma Village
  • Underwater City
  • Fountain City

At Level 700, complete the required progression and enter the Second Sea.

⚡ Second Sea (Level 700-1500)

This is where the game opens up with trading, raids, awakening and much stronger progression.

  • Reach Kingdom Of Rose.
  • Learn fruit values.
  • Start trading.
  • Awaken Buddha if possible.
  • Farm bosses.
  • Do Factory Raids.
  • Unlock Race V2.
  • Unlock Race V3.
  • Collect fragments.
  • Farm Mastery.
  • Build a strong inventory.

Do not spend all your resources randomly. Focus on upgrades that will help you reach the Third Sea efficiently.

💰 Trading Guide

Never trade based only on rarity. Learn actual demand, market value and whether the other player is offering a fair deal.

  • Kitsune — Extremely Valuable
  • Dragon — Extremely Valuable
  • Leopard — Very Valuable
  • Dough — High Demand
  • T-Rex — Good Value
  • Mammoth — Good Value
  • Spirit — Good Value
  • Control — Collector Value

⚠️ Always check current values before making important trades.

🌋 Third Sea (Level 1500+)

Third Sea contains some of the strongest players, best items and hardest challenges.

  • Port Town
  • Hydra Island
  • Great Tree
  • Floating Turtle
  • Haunted Castle
  • Sea Of Treats

This is where much of the advanced and endgame progression takes place.

🥋 Get Godhuman

Godhuman is one of the most popular advanced fighting styles for PvP and combo builds.

  • Unlock Electric Claw.
  • Unlock Dragon Talon.
  • Unlock Sharkman Karate.
  • Unlock Death Step.
  • Unlock Superhuman.
  • Meet the required mastery and material requirements.
  • Purchase Godhuman.

This takes significant grinding, so collect materials and mastery along the way.

🎸 Get Soul Guitar

  • Reach Third Sea.
  • Complete the required puzzle steps.
  • Collect the required materials.
  • Complete the Soul Guitar puzzle.
  • Purchase Soul Guitar.

Soul Guitar is a powerful gun that is especially useful in advanced combat and PvP builds.

⚔️ Get Cursed Dual Katana

  • Get Yama.
  • Get Tushita.
  • Complete the required sword trials.
  • Finish the CDK puzzle.
  • Unlock Cursed Dual Katana.

CDK is one of the most recognizable endgame swords and is widely used in PvP builds.

🌈 Race V4

  • Reach Third Sea.
  • Find Mirage Island.
  • Obtain Blue Gear.
  • Visit Temple Of Time.
  • Complete the required trials.
  • Awaken Race V4.

Race V4 provides powerful abilities and is an important milestone for endgame players.

🐉 Leviathan Hunt

One of the major late-game activities requiring preparation and teamwork.

  • Build a strong team.
  • Prepare for dangerous sea encounters.
  • Search for Leviathan.
  • Defeat Leviathan.
  • Collect and use the rewards.

👑 Endgame Checklist

🏆 If you complete most of the goals above, you've reached the core endgame progression of Blox Fruits.
