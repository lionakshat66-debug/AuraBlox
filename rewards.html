🏆 Blox Fruits Rewards

Complete guide to rewards, bonuses and progression

🎁 What Are Rewards?

Rewards help players level up faster, earn money, reset stats, unlock titles and gain useful bonuses. They can come from redeem codes, quests, raids, bosses, events and special activities.

⚡ 2x EXP Boost

One of the most valuable rewards in the game. EXP boosts allow you to level up much faster while completing quests and defeating enemies.

Best For: Grinding Levels

🔄 Stat Reset

Stat resets allow you to redistribute your stats. This is useful if you want to switch from a sword build to a fruit build or experiment with new setups.

Best For: Changing Builds

💰 Beli

Beli is the main currency in Blox Fruits. It can be used to buy fruits, weapons, fighting styles and other useful items.

Best For: Shopping & Upgrades

📰 Titles

Some rewards unlock special titles. Titles are mainly cosmetic and show off achievements or code rewards.

Example: BIGNEWS Title

👑 Boss Rewards

Bosses can drop rare accessories, weapons and materials. Many powerful items in the game come from boss drops.

  • Accessories
  • Rare Weapons
  • Materials
  • Fragments

🌊 Sea Rewards

Progressing through the seas unlocks stronger enemies, better rewards and access to advanced content.

  • First Sea - Basic Progression
  • Second Sea - Trading & Raids
  • Third Sea - Endgame Content

🐉 Special Event Rewards

Some events and updates include limited-time rewards such as titles, boosts and rare items. These rewards are often only available during specific updates.

💡 Reward Tips

  • Save EXP boosts for long grinding sessions.
  • Don't waste stat resets unless needed.
  • Farm bosses for rare drops.
  • Check for new redeem codes after updates.
  • Complete quests to earn extra rewards.
